Low-bandwidth classroom edge · hackathon prototype

Turn the lecture into text at the source.

A full sentence is ~20 bytes. A second of audio is 100× that. Lekha runs ASR, notes, and translation on one classroom device and streams only captions to student phones — over local Wi-Fi, even when the tower is on 2G.

Open edge console → Join as student

Vs. 1s audio ~100× smaller on the wire

Classroom edge device — all heavy AI stays here

USB mic→ Silero VAD→ Whisper.cpp→ text
Job A · <2s

Live captions, 30–80 byte packets, QoS-priority for deaf students.

Job C · batched

Translate only languages someone in the room subscribed to.
